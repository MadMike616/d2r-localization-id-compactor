The upstream game-data TXT resources and test/save fixtures are not distributed.
Local changes expose read-only runeword/source ID bit offsets and item-section end
positions, and add stat-decoding errors with the preceding stat's bit widths.
No save serializer is used
by the utility's repair actions. The utility patches original bytes directly.
